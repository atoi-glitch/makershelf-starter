Want an EverBee alternative without paying for research features you won't use? Start with eRank for Etsy SEO, Alura for a broader shop toolkit, or Etsy Marketplace Insights for first-party keyword data inside Etsy.

1. eRank — best alternative for Etsy SEO on a budget

If you've already decided what to sell and want more help getting existing listings discovered, eRank is the clearest place to start. It emphasizes keyword research, listing audits, shop data, and competitor monitoring rather than EverBee's product-first workflow.

  • Free: 5 keyword lookups and 5 listing audits a day, subject to plan limits.
  • Basic: $5.99/month monthly or $65.99/year, with higher research limits and competitor monitoring.
  • Trade-off: choose EverBee instead if estimated product-level sales and revenue research are the primary reason you need a tool.

2. Alura — best alternative for an all-in-one Etsy workflow

Alura combines research with listing optimization and seller operations. Its platform includes keyword and product/shop research, limited optimization tools on Free, and higher-tier features that extend into marketing and analytics.

  • Free: up to 5 daily searches per tool and limited research/optimization access.
  • Basic: $7.99/month equivalent with annual billing, or $11.99 billed monthly.
  • Trade-off: an all-in-one platform may feel unnecessary if you only want a few keywords per week.

3. Etsy Marketplace Insights — best free first-party option

Don't overlook a tool you may already have as an Etsy seller. Marketplace Insights uses actual Etsy search activity and shows keyword demand and listing competition from the recent 30-day period.

  • Free usage: 15 keyword searches each week, according to Etsy's help documentation.
  • Access: Shop Manager → Stats → Marketplace Insights, on Etsy's web interface.
  • Trade-off: it is not a replacement for estimated listing-level sales data or a full competitor research suite.

Is EverBee still worth using?

Yes, for the right job. EverBee's free Hobby plan includes limited product analytics and keyword research. Its paid Growth plan adds sales and revenue estimates, trend/growth features, unlimited keyword research, and advanced filters. If product validation is your daily task, switching solely to save on the monthly bill may not help.

Keep EverBee if research into individual products and demand signals matters more than listing audits. Consider switching when most of your time is spent optimizing existing listings or when your free tool limits already cover your needs.

How to choose an EverBee alternative in 10 minutes

  1. Write down your actual bottleneck: finding products, picking keywords, or running your shop.
  2. Try the free option matching that bottleneck. Do the same small research task in each tool.
  3. Record whether the result changes a real listing or product decision.
  4. Pay only when a specific limit prevents you from completing the job.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest EverBee alternative?

eRank and Alura offer free plans. Etsy Marketplace Insights also allows free weekly keyword searches for sellers with access. For a paid SEO-focused plan, eRank Basic has a lower published starting price than EverBee Growth.

Is eRank better than EverBee?

Not universally. eRank is generally the more SEO-oriented option; EverBee puts more emphasis on product analytics and validation. See our EverBee vs eRank comparison.

Can I research Etsy products for free?

Yes, to a point. EverBee and Alura offer limited free research options, while Etsy's own tool provides free buyer keyword research. Free plans will not necessarily show the same depth of estimated sales data as paid product research tools.

Bottom line

Use eRank for SEO, Alura for a broader workflow, and Etsy Marketplace Insights for free first-party keywords. If your priority is validating product demand through estimated sales and revenue, EverBee itself might remain the better fit.
