4 Best Etsy SEO Tools for 2026

The best Etsy SEO tool depends on what you are actually trying to improve. eRank is the strongest all-around choice for everyday Etsy SEO, EverBee is better if product research matters just as much as keywords, Alura is useful if you want a broader all-in-one seller toolkit, and Marmalead is built around deeper keyword research and listing optimization.

1. eRank — Best overall Etsy SEO tool

If your main goal is improving how your existing listings perform in Etsy search, eRank is the easiest recommendation for most sellers. Its toolkit centers on keyword research, listing audits, competitor tracking, trend data and shop health.

eRank also has a free tier, which makes it useful for learning Etsy SEO before paying. Its current paid plans start at a relatively low price, with Basic listed at $5.50 per month when billed annually.

Best for

  • Keyword research
  • Listing audits
  • Rank and competitor tracking
  • Sellers who already have products listed
  • Budget-conscious Etsy shops

Skip it if

Your biggest question is not “How do I optimize this listing?” but “What should I sell next?” In that case, EverBee may be a better starting point.

2. EverBee — Best for SEO + product research

EverBee overlaps with eRank on keyword research, but its biggest advantage is product research. Its Growth plan includes sales and revenue estimates, conversion-rate data, trends, unlimited keyword research and advanced filters.

That makes EverBee especially useful when SEO is only one part of your problem. If you are trying to identify promising products, study demand and then optimize your listings around what you find, EverBee offers a more research-heavy workflow.

Best for

  • Finding new Etsy product ideas
  • Studying competing listings
  • Trend and demand research
  • Keyword research tied to product validation

Skip it if

You only need basic keyword research and listing audits. eRank's lower-cost plans may be enough.

Best if product research matters too

3. Alura — Best all-in-one Etsy seller toolkit

Alura is broader than a traditional SEO tool. Its current platform combines keyword research, listing optimization, product and shop research, analytics, AI-assisted listing work, customer follow-ups, Etsy Ads optimization and even Pinterest marketing features on higher plans.

That makes it interesting for sellers who would rather use one platform for several parts of running an Etsy shop instead of combining multiple specialist tools.

Best for

  • Sellers who want SEO and operational tools in one place
  • Listing optimization with AI assistance
  • Etsy sellers who also want analytics and marketing tools

Skip it if

You want a very focused, inexpensive SEO tool. eRank is simpler if keyword and listing optimization are your main needs.

4. Marmalead — Best for keyword-focused SEO research

Marmalead is one of the long-running Etsy SEO platforms and now combines its keyword research tools with AI features. Its current toolkit includes search-volume data, engagement estimates, seasonality, keyword comparisons, listing grades and AI-assisted listing writing.

It is a stronger fit for sellers who actively want to spend time studying keywords and improving listings, rather than someone who simply wants the cheapest possible SEO helper.

Best for

  • Deeper keyword analysis
  • Seasonal keyword planning
  • Listing grading and optimization
  • Established sellers who take SEO seriously

Skip it if

You are brand new and want to experiment for free first. Start with eRank, EverBee or Alura's free options before adding another paid subscription.

Do you need a paid Etsy SEO tool?

Not necessarily. If you are new, start free. A paid tool only becomes useful when you know what limitation is slowing you down: too few keyword searches, missing competitor data, limited product research or too much manual listing work.

Paying for multiple tools before you have a clear need usually adds cost without improving your shop. Test one free option, build a repeatable workflow, and upgrade only when the paid feature solves a specific problem.

Our recommendation

For most Etsy sellers, start with eRank. It gives you the clearest path from keyword research to listing optimization without requiring a large monthly budget.

If you are still researching what to sell, start with EverBee instead. Its product analytics make it better suited to validating demand before you spend time creating a new product.
