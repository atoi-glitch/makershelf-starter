EverBee vs eRank: Which Is Better for Etsy Sellers?

Short answer: choose EverBee if product research and sales/revenue insights are your priority. Choose eRank if you want deeper day-to-day Etsy SEO, listing audits and competitor tracking at a lower entry price.

EverBee is better for product research

EverBee is built around finding and validating product opportunities. Its paid Growth plan adds estimated sales, revenue, conversion-rate data, trend information, unlimited keyword research and advanced filters. The free Hobby plan lets you explore product analytics with limits and does not require a credit card.

If your biggest question is “What should I sell?”, EverBee is usually the more natural starting point.

eRank is better for ongoing Etsy SEO work

eRank puts more emphasis on keyword research, listing audits, rank checking, traffic insights and competitor monitoring. Its paid plans also start much lower than EverBee's premium research plan, which makes it attractive for sellers who mainly want to improve existing listings rather than research new product opportunities.

If your biggest question is “How do I help my existing listings get found?”, eRank is usually the better fit.

Pricing

EverBee

  • Hobby: $0/month.
  • Growth: $39/month when paid monthly, or $299/year (about $24.99/month).
  • Business: $69/month when billed annually.

eRank

eRank has a free membership. Its current annual-plan equivalents start at $5.50/month for Basic, $7.92/month for Pro and $22.50/month for Expert. Annual billing applies to those figures.

Which one should you choose?

CHOOSE EVERBEE IF

You are still deciding what to sell

You want estimated product sales/revenue data, trend discovery and product validation to play a central role.

CHOOSE ERANK IF

You already have listings to improve

You care most about keywords, listing audits, rank tracking and competitor monitoring without a large monthly spend.

Can you use both?

Yes. Their strengths overlap, but they are not identical. A seller could use EverBee for product discovery and validation, then use eRank for ongoing keyword and listing work. But beginners do not need to pay for both. Start with the free plans and upgrade only when a specific limitation gets in your way.

Our recommendation

For a brand-new seller who is still looking for product opportunities, start with EverBee's free Hobby plan. For a seller with an established catalog who mainly wants to improve SEO, start with eRank's free membership. Only upgrade once you can name the paid feature you actually need.
