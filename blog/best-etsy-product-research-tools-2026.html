4 Best Etsy Product Research Tools for 2026

If your biggest question is “What should I sell on Etsy?”, product research tools can help you compare demand, competition, listings and trends before you spend hours creating. EverBee is the strongest option for sales and revenue estimates, EHunt is useful for broad database research, Alura combines research with listing and marketing tools, and eRank is a budget-friendly option when SEO matters just as much as product discovery.

1. EverBee — Best overall for product validation

EverBee is the easiest starting point if you want product research to answer a practical question: is this idea showing real signs of demand? Its product analytics focus on listing-level sales and revenue estimates, conversion data, trend direction, growth rate and competitor research.

The free Hobby plan lets you explore product analytics with limits, while the Growth plan adds sales and revenue data, unlimited keyword research, trends and advanced filters.

Best for

  • Validating product ideas before creating them
  • Comparing estimated sales and revenue
  • Spotting trends and fast-growing listings
  • Digital products, POD and handmade sellers

Skip it if

You mostly need listing SEO and keyword audits on a very small budget. eRank may be enough.

2. EHunt — Best for broad database research

EHunt, previously known as EtsyHunt, is built around product, keyword and shop databases. It is useful when you want to scan a large number of listings, niches and shops rather than focus only on a few individual products.

Its toolkit includes product research, keyword analysis, shop analysis, listing optimization and data tracking. That makes it particularly useful for sellers who research many niches or want to compare categories at scale.

Best for

  • Bulk product research
  • Niche discovery
  • Competitor and shop analysis
  • Sellers who want a large research database

Skip it if

You want the simplest interface and your main focus is estimated sales and revenue on individual listings. EverBee is more focused on that workflow.

Visit EHunt

3. Alura — Best all-in-one research workflow

Alura combines product and keyword research with listing optimization, analytics, AI tools, customer follow-ups, Etsy Ads optimization and Pinterest marketing on higher plans.

Its free plan includes limited daily searches and research database access, so it is easy to test before paying. This makes Alura a good fit if you want one platform to handle research and several other parts of running your Etsy shop.

Best for

  • Product and keyword research in one platform
  • Listing optimization
  • Sellers who want research plus marketing tools
  • Growing shops that want fewer separate subscriptions

Skip it if

You only want a specialist product research tool. EverBee or EHunt may feel more direct.

Visit Alura

4. eRank — Best low-cost research + SEO option

eRank is better known for Etsy SEO, but it can still be useful during product research because keyword demand, competition, trends and competitor tracking help you judge whether a niche is worth exploring.

Its biggest advantage is price. The free plan is useful for testing the workflow, and the Basic annual plan starts at a much lower monthly equivalent than many specialist research platforms.

Best for

  • Keyword-led product research
  • Trend and competitor monitoring
  • Sellers who also need listing SEO
  • Small budgets

Skip it if

Your decision depends heavily on estimated listing revenue and sales. EverBee is more purpose-built for that.

Do you need a paid product research tool?

Not at first. Use the free plans to learn what data actually changes your decisions. Upgrade only when a limitation stops you from researching efficiently, such as capped searches, missing revenue estimates or limited competitor tracking.

A research tool should help you avoid wasting time on weak product ideas. If it simply gives you more dashboards without changing what you create, you probably do not need the paid plan yet.

Our recommendation

For most sellers who are specifically trying to decide what to sell, start with EverBee. Its product-focused data makes it easier to compare ideas and validate demand. If budget matters more and you want SEO tools too, start with eRank.
