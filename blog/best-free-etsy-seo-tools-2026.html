3 Best Free Etsy SEO Tools for 2026

If you are new to Etsy SEO, you do not need to pay for a subscription right away. eRank, EverBee and Alura all let you start for free, but they are useful for different jobs. Here is the fastest way to choose the right one without wasting money.

1. eRank — Best free Etsy SEO tool overall

eRank is the strongest starting point if your main goal is Etsy SEO. Its free membership does not require a credit card and does not have a time limit. The platform focuses on keyword research, listing analysis, trend research and competitive insights.

For most new sellers, that is enough to learn the basics of Etsy SEO before paying for anything.

Use the free plan if you want to

  • Research Etsy keywords
  • Check listing SEO
  • Explore trends
  • Learn which terms shoppers may be searching for

Upgrade only when

You repeatedly hit the free limits or need more competitor tracking, connected listings, daily keyword searches or advanced listing analysis.

2. EverBee — Best free option for product research

EverBee is different from a pure SEO tool because it combines keyword research with product research. Its Hobby plan is free forever and does not require a credit card.

The free plan includes limited product analytics, basic filters and up to 10 keyword searches. That makes it a good fit when your biggest question is not just “Which keyword should I use?” but “What kind of product should I make?”

Use the free plan if you want to

  • Explore product ideas
  • See views, reviews and favorites on listings
  • Try basic filters
  • Run a small number of keyword searches

Upgrade only when

You need sales and revenue estimates, unlimited keyword research, trend data, advanced filters or larger saved-research limits.

Best free option for research-first sellers
Try EverBee free

3. Alura — Best free all-in-one option

Alura lets Etsy sellers create a free account without entering credit card details. It is broader than a single-purpose SEO tool and is designed around a wider shop-growth workflow, including research and listing optimization.

That makes Alura useful if you want to test a more all-in-one approach before deciding whether you need a paid seller platform.

Use the free plan if you want to

  • Explore an all-in-one Etsy toolkit
  • Test research and listing workflows
  • See whether a broader platform fits the way you work

Upgrade only when

You know which paid features you would actually use on a regular basis. If your needs are mostly keyword research, eRank may remain the simpler choice.

Do free Etsy SEO tools actually work?

They can absolutely be enough for a beginner. A tool does not rank your listings for you. What matters is whether it helps you make better decisions about keywords, products and listings.

The best approach is to use one free tool consistently, learn what data is useful to you, and only pay once you know exactly which limitation is costing you time or opportunities.

Our recommendation

Start with eRank if you already have listings and want to improve SEO. Start with EverBee if you are still deciding what to sell. Try Alura if you prefer a broader seller platform.

There is no reason for a new Etsy seller to pay for all three.
