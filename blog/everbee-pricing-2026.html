Is EverBee free?

The Hobby plan lets you explore basic product analytics and keyword research with limits. It is a useful starting point for learning how research works and exploring product ideas without a subscription.

EverBee free vs paid: what's the difference?

Growth adds more detailed estimated sales, revenue and conversion-rate data, trend information, advanced filters and more extensive keyword research. Competitor sales and revenue figures are estimates, not verified financial results.

STAY FREE IF

You are exploring ideas

CONSIDER GROWTH IF

You research products regularly

You need deeper market data to validate new products and compare opportunities.

Is EverBee Growth worth it?

At $39 per month, the subscription should provide enough practical value to justify its cost. If your contribution margin is $8 per sale, five additional sales would cover the monthly subscription. A research tool does not guarantee additional sales.

Consider upgrading when you regularly launch products, research multiple niches or reach the limits of the free plan.
